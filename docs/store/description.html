Artificial Chaos

Artificial Chaos is a 2D top-down squad-combat game prototype built with pygame-ce. A rogue AI called ARGUS has seized human infrastructure and, through it, human minds — soldiers across the map are still alive, still human, but dormant, standing idle until something snaps them out of it. You're the Squad Leader, the last person still in control of your own mind (an off-grid forward position kept you outside ARGUS's mass suppression signal), and your commlink still runs pre-ARGUS firmware — which is why simply walking up to a dormant soldier is enough to free them.

Gameplay

Move the Squad Leader around the map. Get close to a dormant soldier and they join your army, following you while spreading out to avoid crowding each other. Hold the left mouse button to fire your sidearm at the nearest of ARGUS's combat drones in range — ranged attacks flash a muzzle, a tracer flies to the target, and a spark/blood-spatter hits home; drones burst into an explosion on death. Recruited soldiers auto-fight nearby drones too, entirely on their own — there's no per-unit targeting, every soldier picks its own nearest threat. Every objective flag spawns with a guardian drone and keeps reinforcing itself with more while it stays uncaptured; clear the area, then hold the flag (with the Squad Leader or a recruited soldier, uncontested by a nearby drone) until it locks in. Capture every flag on the map to win; the Squad Leader dying ends the run. Capturing a flag also ranks you up, buffing your HP, speed, fire rate, or damage.

Two mechanics turn squad combat into an active decision instead of something that just happens in the background: your soldiers only actually land a hit while you've stopped moving (they still aim the instant a target's in range, they just don't connect until you hold still) — and Tab toggles the whole squad between Engage (spread out and fight independently) and Guard (a tight escort formation that ignores threats far from you). Neither ever lets you pick who a soldier shoots at — that's a deliberate pillar of the game, not a missing feature.

Entities

Entity Sprite(s) Behaviour
Squad Leader (player) SquadLeader.png WASD/arrow movement with friction, footprint trail, recruits nearby soldiers, rank insignia, fires a sidearm at the nearest drone in range, toggles squad stance
Soldier (6 classes) Assault, Sniper, MachineGunner, AntiTank, Grenadier, RadioOperator Recruited on proximity, then follows/avoids crowding and auto-engages the nearest drone in range; Grenadier throws splash-damage grenades, RadioOperator calls in reinforcements instead of fighting
Drone (5 types, ARGUS's combat arm) Scarab, Spider, Hornet, Wasp, Centipede Aggros within range, chases, then melees or fires; Hornet kites at range instead of holding ground; Centipede is a genuinely segmented multi-part body
Objective flag objective-flag.png, objective-pulse.png Spawns a guardian drone and keeps reinforcing while uncaptured; fills in while held and uncontested, locks in once captured and grants a rank-up

Status

Prototype, fully playable start to finish: all 6 soldier classes, all 5 drone types, flag-capture win/lose, rank-up bonuses, and a first pass of combat effects and balance are in. Built on my shared pygamine engine (vendored as a git submodule, like chokepoint).

Requirements

  • Python 3.12+
  • pygame-ce, pytmx (resolved automatically from pyproject.toml / uv.lock)
  • uv (optional but recommended)

Running the Game (Source)

git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/umutcanekinci/artifical-chaos.git
cd artifical-chaos
uv sync
uv run python __main__.py

If you forgot --recurse-submodules: git submodule update --init.

Without uv: pip install . then python __main__.py.

Project Layout

__main__.py            Entry point — injects src/ + src/pygamine/ onto sys.path, runs Game()
src/app/game.py         Game (extends pygamine.Application) — update/draw orchestration
src/gameplay/map.py     Tiled map loader, obstacles, flag/drone/soldier spawning
src/gameplay/player.py  Squad Leader — movement, sidearm, rank-ups, squad stance toggle
src/gameplay/soldier.py Recruitable soldiers (6 classes) — follow/avoid/auto-engage
src/gameplay/robot.py   Drone base class + 5 concrete types (aggro/chase/attack AI)
src/gameplay/flag.py    Objective flags — capture progress, contest, reinforcement spawner
src/gameplay/combat.py  Shared hitscan combat primitives
src/gameplay/effects.py Cosmetic combat VFX (muzzle flash, tracer, explosions, smoke, ...)
src/util/constants.py   Tuning constants (stats, ranges, cooldowns, rank bonuses)
src/pygamine/        Shared engine (git submodule)
assets/                 Images (soldiers, robots, effects, UI), tileset + Tiled map
